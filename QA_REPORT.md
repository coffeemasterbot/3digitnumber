## State and money

The original `life-lost-found-guided-v5` localStorage key remains, with schema version 6 and validated migration from version 5. Story state `s` stores chapters/unlocks, collected clues, witnesses, solved financial questions, time, dialogue and branch choices, budget assumptions, habit dates/stamps, and ending ID. Demo state remains in memory. Original audio and standalone game record keys remain separate. Nothing is uploaded.

`JourneyCore.project(s)` is the single financial calculation: integer cents for starting cash, expected salary, food, transport, vouchers, phone bill and social commitment. Its rows feed existing guided budget, ledger, download and ending logic. Current cash is HK$50; the HK$600 salary is expected income on Day 3 or Day 5 in the projection. Negative projected balances remain explicit funding gaps; acknowledging support never creates income or erases a gap. This is a plan, not a live payment simulation. Scenario purchases do not debit the story's starting bank balance.

The range has one reducer-owned in-memory state, no storage or story-state access, and 900 fictional credits across all three rounds. It sends only an exit message. The parent validates message origin and frame identity. Exiting removes the entire frame; pagehide aborts listeners, cancels animation and closes its audio context.

## Controls and scoring

Desktop: mouse click shoots/rejects a target; numbered text cards select it; X shoots, G protects, I checks, R reloads, P pauses. On the focused canvas, arrows move the crosshair and Space fires. Tab/Enter plus the action buttons provide a full keyboard path. Touch: tap the range to shoot, or select a readable text card and tap reject/protect/check/reload. Untimed mode uses exactly the same nine scenarios and score rules.

Practice is unscored and free. Timed rounds last 45 seconds, with an optional 15-second card. Each round resets to a free 3-shot marker; rentals last only that round and do not refund. Stable marker: 180 credits, five shots, larger hit area, slower firing. Rapid marker: 240, eight shots, faster firing/reload, smaller hit area. Time card: 120; disabled in untimed mode. Purchase validation blocks repeats, incompatible markers and insufficient budget. No credit replenishment or story rewards exist.

Financial score = rounded correct judgements / 9 × 80, plus rounded credits left / 900 × 20. Uncertain offers require checking before rejection/protection; guessing receives no judgement credit. Unhandled timed-out targets are not correct. Shooting accuracy = target hits / shots, displayed separately and excluded from the financial score. Each round explains every target, including missed decisions. The debrief connects essential needs, verified information and future resources to the main story.
